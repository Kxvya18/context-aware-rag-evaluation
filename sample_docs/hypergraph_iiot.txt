Hypergraph spectral analysis can model coordinated attacks in Industrial Internet of Things networks. Unlike pairwise graphs, hypergraphs represent simultaneous multi-device interactions as hyperedges. This helps capture higher-order attack patterns where multiple compromised devices coordinate together.

Normalized hypergraph Laplacian concepts and spectral perturbation analysis can help distinguish benign topology changes from coordinated attack behavior. The project connects IIoT security, intrusion detection, cyber-physical systems, network science, hypergraph theory, spectral analysis, and mathematical modeling.
